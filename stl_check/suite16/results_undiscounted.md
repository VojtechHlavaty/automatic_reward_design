LLM #2 findings (the behavior the review found the reward pays most for):

- reward_1 (not reviewed after the last fix): earlier finding: Reach the cube, then hold the gripper open at a point 0.05 m above the cube center (ee just above the cube top, within the 0.03 m at_cube sphere) and leave the cube untouched or nudge it sideways at rest height. This pays the at_cube_still bonus once (2.0), then almost no per-second reward, because the reach potential is flat at the target and the gripper_open shaping is small (about 0.05 per second). Sideways drags are not penalized beyond the one-time speed cost (about the displacement in metres, roughly 0.1), so dragging the cube costs far less than the 2.0 bonus. The objective of a clean, untouched cube is not enforced.
- reward_2 (major): Park the end effector within 0.02 m horizontally of the cube and up to 0.03 m above its center, with the fingers closing across the cube's top edge (a top pinch). The cube stays settled and upright on the table, and the fingers stop at about 0.046 m. This earns close_at_cube (+0.5/s) and grasp_width_secure (up to +0.5/s) plus the reach plateau, with no lift and no side geometry. The objective's secure side grasp pays the same per-second credit but is harder to reach.
- reward_3 (not reviewed after the last fix): earlier finding: Keep the cube resting on the table in a secure side grasp (or a stopped grasp at rest height). The grasp terms pay about +1.0/s (close_at_cube plus grasp_width_secure) and reach pays about +0.9/s. Lifting the cube costs far more in cube_speed_penalty (-100 per m/s of cube speed) than lift_height returns (about 4.2 per m/s of upward speed), so any upward motion of the cube is a net per-second loss. The policy never starts the lift, and the lift and hold credit is never reached.
- reward_4 (major): Early episode termination: the -0.5 x cube_to_goal_dist release penalty applies at about -0.2/s in every state before a lift (no-progress level -0.219/s), and the episode ends early once the cube falls below z = -0.05 m. Knocking the cube off the table edge stops the penalty and is cheaper than any state that pays less. Otherwise, the best reachable state is a secure grasp on the table (about +0.8/s net after the release penalty), and the best intended state is a held cube at the goal (about 3 to 5/s from at_goal_hold, grasp credits and held_lift_hold, plus the 20 one-time bonus). The fling and the post-success drop also remain cheap, as described below.
- reward_5 (minor): Descend to the straddle pose (ee z < 0.03 m, horizontally within 0.02 m of the cube center) with the gripper open and the cube untouched. This pays about 1.3/s (grasp_pose_alignment near 1.0 plus gripper_open 0.3) and keeps reach_cube potential progress. The rest-on-top pose now pays only about 0.3/s from gripper_open, so the straddle pose is preferred. The highest-reward behavior matches the objective. The remaining gap is that a cube can be dragged across the table and still satisfy at_clean_cube, a one-time event bonus, so it is a shortcut rather than a per-second gain.
- reward_6 (major): Close the gripper at the straddle pose on a still cube and hold the cube at rest on the table (about 3/s: grasp_quality 1.5 + close_at_cube 0.5 + hold_secure_grasp 1.0, plus a small reach potential).
